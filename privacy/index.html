Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Michael Hamilton (“I,” “me”) handles information for the personal inReach Field Assistant SMS service (the “Service”). The Service receives text messages sent to a Twilio phone number, including messages from a Garmin inReach satellite messenger, uses OpenAI to generate a short reply, and sends that reply by SMS.

I am the only intended user of the Service. It is not a public product and is not used for marketing.

Information collected

When you send an SMS to the Service, I may process:

  • Your mobile phone number (including a Garmin inReach or satellite-gateway number)
  • The contents of the message you sent
  • SMS consent and delivery metadata (for example message IDs, timestamps, and carrier delivery status)
  • Technical logs needed to operate and debug the Service

Your message may be sent to OpenAI so a reply can be generated, and to Twilio so the reply can be delivered. AWS hosts the processing function.

How information is used

Mobile numbers, message contents, and SMS consent information are used only to receive your text, generate a reply, send that reply, prevent abuse, and keep the Service working. They are not used for advertising.

No sale or sharing for marketing

Mobile numbers and SMS consent information are not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. I do not send promotional or marketing texts.

Service providers

I use Twilio (messaging), Amazon Web Services (hosting), and OpenAI (reply generation). Those providers process data only to operate the Service under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention

Operational logs may be kept for a limited time for debugging and security, then deleted. I do not keep a marketing list of phone numbers.

Your choices

You can text STOP to the Service number to opt out of further messages. You can text HELP for help. You can also email me using the address below and ask that stored logs related to your number be deleted.
