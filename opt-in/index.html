How to opt in to SMS

This page is the public call-to-action for the inReach Field Assistant messaging program. Reviewers can verify the full consent flow here.

Brand: inReach Field Assistant

What you get: one informational SMS reply after you send a question (weather, trail conditions, water sources, fire restrictions, road closures, town hours). Not marketing.

How you opt in: text START, YES, or UNSTOP to the inReach Field Assistant 10DLC number listed on this campaign. You can also opt in by sending your first question to that number after saving it as an SMS contact.

Message frequency varies (typically one reply per inbound question). Message and data rates may apply, including satellite message credits if you use a satellite messenger.

Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to cancel. After STOP you receive a confirmation and no further messages unless you opt in again.

Privacy Policy (mobile numbers are not shared with third parties for marketing) · Terms & Conditions

Confirmation you receive

After a keyword opt-in, the program sends:

“inReach Field Assistant: You're opted in. I'll reply to your satellite texts. Msg frequency varies. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.”

Help and opt-out

HELP reply: “inReach Field Assistant: For help email mike.hamilton92@gmail.com. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.”

STOP reply: “inReach Field Assistant: You are opted out and will not receive further messages. Reply START to opt back in.”
